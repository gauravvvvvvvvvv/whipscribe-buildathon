WhipScribe · Track 1 · Challenge 01

The transcript should feel like the content, not the controls.

My next pass optimizes for one mobile job: find the moment, understand who said it, and keep listening without losing your place.

I deliberately kept the existing visual language. This is a product-structure pass, not a rebrand: less sticky chrome, one-tap search, an honest processing state, usable multi-speaker reading, and contextual actions that appear only when they are needed.

What I deliberately kept.

The goal is not to redraw everything. The current pass already has useful structure.

Timestamps in the gutter

They connect reading to playback and give scan anchors. I would keep them visible instead of hiding them behind a settings toggle by default.

A persistent audio player

Reading and listening are the same task here. The player only yields space while the keyboard or a blocking sheet is active.

Transcript as a first-class tab

Summary and Ask are useful, but the transcript is the evidence. It remains a peer surface rather than becoming buried under AI features.

Desktop unchanged

This pass is intentionally mobile-only. None of these decisions require changing the laptop layout, which reduces scope and regression risk.

Implementation notes: this is deliberately a static product prototype, not a fake production implementation. The processing screen avoids unsupported percentages/ETAs. Search keeps its own one-tap entry point instead of being hidden in a universal actions menu. The preview CTA is moved into the content boundary so the transcript and player remain the two persistent layers.
